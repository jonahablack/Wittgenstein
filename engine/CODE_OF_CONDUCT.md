# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or experience level, with particular attention to
the norms of empirical, evidence-based research work: good-faith disagreement about
evidence grading, transferability, and epistemic status is expected and welcome;
personal attacks are not.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Grounding disagreements in evidence, citations, and stated limitations.
- Being explicit about uncertainty rather than overstating confidence.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on evidence extraction.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Misrepresenting a source's findings to win an argument about registry inclusion.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers listed in
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.
