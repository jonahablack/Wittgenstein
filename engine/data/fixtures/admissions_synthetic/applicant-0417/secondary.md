# Secondary essay — Applicant 0417

Prompt: Describe a challenge you have faced and what you learned from it.

The hardest semester of my life was spring 2024, when I was my mother's main caregiver during her chemotherapy. I learned to keep a schedule of medications on the refrigerator, to ask her oncologist questions she was too tired to ask, and to accept help from others. It taught me that patients' families carry much of the work of care, and that a good physician notices them.
