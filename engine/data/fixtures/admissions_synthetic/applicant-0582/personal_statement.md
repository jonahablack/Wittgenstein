# Personal statement — Applicant 0582

When I was twelve I read my grandmother's discharge papers to her in the hospital parking lot, translating words I didn't understand myself. She had been told to take her new heart medication twice a day; the label said once. We caught it only because I asked the pharmacist.

That afternoon is why I started the Riverside Health Literacy Project. Most of our learners are not short on intelligence; they are short on time and on anyone who explains things plainly. I want to be a physician who explains things plainly, and who builds clinics where that is the norm rather than the exception.
