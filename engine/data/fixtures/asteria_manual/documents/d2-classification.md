# Asteria Autonomous Systems Certification Manual -- Classification

Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2027. Fictional content -- see `README.md` in this directory's parent for the full research-only notice.

Article 2

Classification requirement

1. A Class R inspection unit shall pass the Structural Load Assessment at a minimum clearance margin of 12 centimetres before deployment, provided that the unit has completed a conformity assessment under Definition D-05.

2. A Class S inspection unit shall pass the Structural Load Assessment at a minimum clearance margin of 8 centimetres before deployment.

Article 3

Exception for legacy units

An inspection unit manufactured before 1 January 2025 is exempt from the clearance margin set out in Article 2, except where the unit has been substantially modified since its original manufacture date.

Article 4

Scope of application

The requirements of Article 2 apply to every Class R inspection unit operating within a restricted-access industrial zone on or after 1 March 2027, regardless of the unit's manufacturer. See Section 1 for applicable definitions.
