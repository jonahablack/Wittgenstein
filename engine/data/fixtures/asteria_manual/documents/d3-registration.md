Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2027. Fictional content -- see `README.md` in this directory's parent for the full research-only notice.

Article 5

Registration obligation

1. Before deployment of a Class R inspection unit, the operator shall register the unit with the Asteria Standards Authority in the Unit Registry referenced in Annex I.

2. Before deployment of a Class S inspection unit, the operator shall register the unit with the Asteria Standards Authority in the Unit Registry, unless the unit is deployed for fewer than 5 operating days in a reporting period.

3. A registered operator shall renew registration annually, no later than the last day of the reporting period defined in Definition D-06.

4. A registered operator is subject to the renewal obligation under this Article only if the operator's inspection unit operates in a restricted-access industrial zone.

Article 6

Reporting deadlines

An operator that discovers a nonconformance shall report it to the Asteria Standards Authority within 10 business days, in accordance with the registration obligation set out in Article 5(1). A report under this clause shall cite the applicable row of Table A.2.
